Welcome to Zorig Chusum

Zorig Chusum refers to the thirteen traditional arts and crafts of Bhutan. These skills have been passed down through generations and are an important part of Bhutanese culture and heritage.


Painting

Traditional Bhutanese paintings and religious artwork.

 Wood Carving

Beautiful designs carved into traditional wooden objects.

 Weaving

Traditional textiles made with colorful patterns.
Sculpture

Creating statues and figures using traditional methods.

 Blacksmithing

Making useful tools and objects from metal.

 Pottery

Creating traditional containers and decorative objects.

Why is Zorig Chusum Important?

Zorig Chusum helps preserve Bhutan's traditional knowledge, skills, culture and identity. It also provides opportunities for artisans to continue their traditional crafts.

  • Preserves Bhutanese culture
  • Protects traditional skills
  • Supports local artisans
  • Promotes Bhutanese heritage
